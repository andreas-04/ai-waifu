### What is it?

Focus Fairy is your digital sidekick, ensuring youre on top of your game, mentally and physically. Our goal was to reinvent how productivity is measured, Focus Fairy takes in a comprehensive list of metrics and gives informed notifications where you might be lacking. Using machine learning the Focus Fairy monitors your screen and classifies your current activity as producive or unproductive. Using computer vision Focus Fairy gains a unique insight on your posture, focus and hydration. Using 11Labs for AI-tts the Focus Fairy will remind you if youre slouching, browsing youtube or if you might be a little thisrty.
